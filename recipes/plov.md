# Plov

A classic Central Asian rice dish with meat and carrots.

**Servings:** 4
**Time:** 1 h 30 min

## Ingredients

- 500 g beef or lamb
- 400 g long-grain rice
- 3 carrots
- 2 onions
- 1 head of garlic
- 100 ml vegetable oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt and black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Rinse the rice several times until the water runs clear, then soak it for 30 minutes.
2. Cut the meat into cubes, slice the onions into half rings and the carrots into thin strips.
3. Heat the oil in a heavy pot and brown the meat on high heat.
4. Add the onions and fry until golden, then add the carrots and fry for 5 more minutes.
5. Season with cumin, salt and pepper, pour in water to cover and simmer for 30 minutes.
6. Drain the rice and spread it evenly over the meat. Do not stir.
7. Add hot water so it covers the rice by 1–2 cm, press the whole garlic head into the middle.
8. Cook on medium heat until the water is absorbed, then cover and cook on low heat for 20 minutes.
9. Stir gently before serving.
